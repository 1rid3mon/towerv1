# Economy inspection — 31 October 2026

Scope: offline formula inspection and targeted reward regressions. This is not a measured long-session balance certification or server-trusted economy.

- Endless damage/fire rate were evaluated from the real `data.js` with a fresh default state, no items, souls, permanent upgrades or loot bonuses. Early upgrade levels do not by themselves introduce an arbitrary high-wave unlock or an exponential jump in attack speed. Observed values: level 1 damage 8 / 0.9 shots per second / 7.2 DPS; level 3: 14 / 1.01 / 14.14; level 5: 20 / 1.12 / 22.4; level 10: 45 / 1.395 / 62.775. These omit crits, travel time and target downtime.
- Normal enemy base HP in `spawnEnemy` scales with wave (1.095 exponent), 10-wave and 25-wave tier factors. Variants and bosses further modify it. Preserve established formulas until real retention/playtime data supports a rebalance.
- A concrete false positive was fixed: the client guard previously rolled back crystal increases above 650 every 400 ms even when a high-wave boss, cargo or milestone legitimately awarded them. All engine crystal reward paths now register their credit; unregistered additions, NaN and negative values remain rejected by the client diagnostic guard. Unit tests cover large and multiple credits and unregistered edits.
- Story starts with an isolated 180-gold mission budget and fixed equipment-free loadout. Mission 9's three defense lines require upgrades in the tested route; actual purchase handlers spend that budget. Story rewards and levels cannot mutate the parent Endless save. The browser campaign exercises purchases rather than injecting free damage or gold.
- Prestige/souls/equipment/companions intentionally multiply damage in Endless; Story excludes those multipliers. Long progression with extreme legal combinations, casino expected value, every achievement/title threshold and all wave/boss variants have not received exhaustive measured playtesting.
- Checksums, client shadow state and Firestore numeric ceilings cannot establish that a reward or score was earned. Production Auth/data synchronization and server-authoritative score/currency validation remain separate open work; do not advertise cheat-proof leaderboards or reset legitimate players based on this audit.

Deterministic VM spawn sample using the real engine: wave 1 green HP 14; wave 10 green HP 236; wave 25 runner HP 1,827; wave 50 green HP 162,100; wave 100 green HP 147,241,556. Variant randomness means these are samples, not an exhaustive table of all enemies. The sample supports investigating save/import/permanent-bonus paths before weakening established enemy scaling; it does not certify every route.
